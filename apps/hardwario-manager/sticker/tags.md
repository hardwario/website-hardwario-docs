# Organise saved devices with tags

Tags label and filter the devices in your saved list, for example by site,
customer, floor, or state of deployment. A device can carry as many tags as you
need, and each tag has its own colour.

---

## Tag a device

1. Open **HARDWARIO Manager** and go to **STICKER → Saved STICKERs**.
2. On a device row, or in its **Detail**, choose **Edit tags**.
3. Pick existing tags or create a new one, then confirm.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-sticker-tags.png" alt="Editing the tags on one saved device" width="320" />

---

## Manage the tag registry

Open the **⋮ menu** on the Saved STICKERs list and choose **Tags**.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tags.png" alt="The Saved STICKERs overflow menu with Tags, Import, Export, Export logs, Delete all logs and Delete" width="320" />

The registry covers your whole list: **add**, **rename**, **recolour**, and
**delete** tags, with a count showing how many devices use each one. Renaming or
recolouring a tag updates it on every device at once.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tags-color.png" alt="Choosing a new colour for a tag in the tag registry" width="320" />

---

## Filter by tag

Back in **Saved STICKERs**, tap the **tag icon** and pick one or more tags to
show only the matching devices. A badge on the icon shows how many filters are
active, and **Clear** removes them all.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tag-filter.png" alt="Filtering the Saved STICKERs list by tag, with one filter active and a Clear action" width="320" />

Filtering works alongside the **Serial or name** search box, so you can narrow by
tag and then search within the result.

:::info Tags travel with an export
Tags are not secret, so they ride along in exports. Tick **Include tags** when
exporting to add a tags column to the CSV; importing unions those tags onto the
matching serials and creates any that do not exist yet. See
[**Saved STICKERs**](./saved-stickers.md).
:::
