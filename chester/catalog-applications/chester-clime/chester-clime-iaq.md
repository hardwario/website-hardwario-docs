# CHESTER Clime IAQ

![CHESTER Clime IAQ](/img/chester/catalog-applications/chester-clime-iaq.webp)

**CHESTER Clime IAQ** is the indoor air quality variant of [**CHESTER Clime**](index.md).

:::info Firmware

This variant runs the **[CHESTER Clime IAQ firmware](../../firmware/chester-clime/chester-clime-iaq.md)**.

:::

## Differences from CHESTER Clime

- The integrated **CHESTER-S1** multisensor replaces the external hygrometer. It measures temperature, humidity, illuminance, CO₂ concentration and atmospheric pressure, and detects motion with a PIR sensor.
- Reports push button presses and gives acoustic and optical feedback.
- The button LED color shows the CO₂ concentration level.
- Runs from external power 6–28 V on **CHESTER-X10** with a Li-Ion backup battery.

## Hardware

The hardware of this application consists of the following ordering codes:

* `CHESTER-M-BCGLS`: Standard mainboard
* `CHESTER-S1-BCMP`: Integrated multi-sensor
* `CHESTER-X10`: External power 6–28 V with Li-Ion battery
* `CHESTER-E7-LP`: Enclosure with SMA pigtail

See [**Ordering Codes**](../../ordering-codes.md) for more details.
