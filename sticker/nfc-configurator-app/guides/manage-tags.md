# Organise saved devices with tags

Tags let you label and filter the STICKERs in your saved list — for example by site, customer, or variant.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-saved-stickers.png" alt="Saved Stickers list with colour-coded tags" width="320" />

## Tag a device

1. Open **HARDWARIO Manager** and go to **STICKER → Saved Stickers**.
2. On a device row (or in its **Detail**), choose **Edit tags**.
3. Pick existing tags or create a new one, then confirm.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-sticker-tags.png" alt="Editing a device's tags" width="320" />

## Manage the tag registry

Open the **⋮ menu** on the Saved Stickers list and choose **Tags** to open the registry.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tags.png" alt="Saved Stickers overflow menu — Tags, Import, Export" width="320" />

In the registry you can **add**, **rename**, **recolour**, or **delete** tags across your whole list — for example, pick a new colour for a tag.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tags-color.png" alt="Recolouring a tag in the tag registry" width="320" />

## Filter by tag

Back in **Saved Stickers**, tap the **tag icon** and pick one or more tags to show only the matching devices; the badge shows how many filters are active, and **Clear** removes them.

<img src="/img/hw-manager/hw-manager-tag-filter.png" alt="Filtering the Saved Stickers list by tag" width="320" />
